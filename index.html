Authentic Craft Provenance

From Raw Forest Timber to Sanctuary Heirloom

Follow the journey of how rough-sawn hardwood logs transform into hand-buffed, tailored living pieces in our Yamare workshop.

Stage 01 • Raw Timber

Seasoned Hardwood Selection

We source certified Grade-A Burma Teak, Sheesham, and White Oak, kiln-seasoned to an exact 8–10% moisture content to withstand Bengaluru weather without warping.

Stage 02 • Precision Joinery

Generational Hand Joinery

Our artisans hand-chisel traditional mortise-and-tenon and sliding dovetail joints. Zero plastic brackets, staples, or weak cam-locks. Built to stay rock-solid for decades.

Stage 03 • Living Heirloom

Hand-Buffed Finish & Reveal

Multiple coats of food-safe beeswax, organic oils, and protective matte finishes bring out deep wood grains, delivered and precision-leveled directly in your residence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commissioning custom furniture with Tree Escape Living.

Yes, absolutely. Over 60% of our bespoke commissions begin with a moodboard, architectural sketch, or Pinterest reference. Our Yamare design team adapts the design to your exact room scale, ergonomic requirements, and timber preference, producing scaled 3D CAD renders before any cutting begins.

Our standard production cycle is typically 3 to 4 weeks from 3D drawing sign-off and timber selection to final white-glove installation. Because every piece is hand-built one at a time using generational joinery rather than automated assembly lines, we prioritize structural permanence over rushed factory output.

We work on a transparent 50/50 model: a 50% booking advance upon final 3D drawing approval (which locks in your kiln-dried timber planks and reserves workshop bench time), and the remaining 50% only upon personal white-glove delivery, leveling, and your final inspection in your home.

During our initial consultation or complimentary in-home site visit, we verify lift and staircase clearances across your Bengaluru property. For high-rise apartments in Sarjapur and Whitefield, our master carpenters construct pieces using precision knock-down mortise joinery that is transported seamlessly in sections and assembled securely in your room.

Every piece includes our written 10-Year Structural & Termite Warranty. All hardwoods (Burma Teak, Indian Sheesham, White Oak) are kiln-seasoned down to an optimal 8–10% moisture content and treated with non-toxic borer-resistant organic sealants to ensure total stability through Bengaluru's monsoon and dry seasons.
